About Build Buddy

A solo web design studio building fast, modern websites for businesses that need to be taken seriously online.

Why Build Buddy

Websites built to do a job.

Most small business websites are either thrown together on a builder in an afternoon, or overbuilt by an agency that disappears after launch. Build Buddy sits in between: the care and craft of a proper build, at a size that fits a small business.

Every project starts with the same question — what is this site actually for? More enquiries, more sales, more credibility? Once that's clear, everything else follows: the structure, the content, the design, the build.

I work directly with you, start to finish. No account managers, no handoffs, no surprise invoices.
